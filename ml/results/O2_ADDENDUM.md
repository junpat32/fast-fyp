**The expectation stated before the run** (the Monte Carlo ranks below the model) **did not hold**: the difference from logistic regression has an interval that includes 0, so the two are statistically tied on the O2 label. Possible reasons, for the discussion: the simulation models directly the mechanism behind most positives (cash running out); it uses up to 6 past months where the model's features use 3 (both look only backwards); and in synthetic data a person's future months resemble their past ones by construction, which favours a method that resamples the past.

## Notes

- **B0 and B2 have identical confusion matrices at every noise level.** This is expected, not an error: both baselines score a row only by its state (band × cushion bucket, 9 states), so at the threshold that maximises F1 they flag the same states, and their 0/1 predictions coincide (checked: identical on all 6,646 medium-noise rows). Their rankings differ, which is why their PR-AUCs differ.
- `O2_REPORT.md` says the comparator was not available because T-15 was not built; T-15 has since been built and accepted as an illustrative scenario (D-79, D-81), and the comparator is §5 above. The report itself is kept as generated, as the record of the pre-registered run.
