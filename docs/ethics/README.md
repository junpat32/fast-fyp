## Things that must stay true (they are what makes this low risk)

- **No real financial data**: participants only see fictional personas and type only the made-up
  sign-up card (CLAUDE.md rule 12, D-10, D-94).
- Study accounts are made for one participant and **deleted after the session** (SR1, D-94).
- Participants are adults aged **18–35**; nobody under 18.
- Answers are linked to a **participant code**, never a name; consent forms are kept separately.
- Taking part or not has **no effect on anyone's grades or relationship** with the researcher.
- The comprehension questions' answer keys are written after the personas exist (T-18); the
  questions themselves are fixed here before any data is collected.
