# DECISIONS: FAST design decision log

Every design choice that differs from the FYP1 report, or that a reader might
question, is recorded here with its reason. **Add an entry before changing any of
these.** Under the Design Science Research Methodology (Peffers et al., 2007) that
the project follows, these refinements are the "evaluate → back to design" iteration.

Status: **Final** = decided by Patrick · **Provisional** = decided for building,
to be confirmed with the supervisor before the O2 run (~25 Oct 2026) ·
**Deviation** = a change made after pre-registration (must give the reason)

| ID | Date | Decision | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | 2026-10-07 | **ML predicts 3-month deterioration**, not the current risk band | In FYP1 the band label was a direct function of DSR, which was also a feature, so any model would score ~100% and prove nothing (circular). Predicting the future from current behaviour gives ML a job rules can't do | Provisional |
| D-02 | 2026-10-07 | Split by user (`GroupKFold`); main metric PR-AUC, plus minority-class recall, macro-F1 and Brier score; class weights instead of SMOTE; user-level bootstrap CIs | User-months are correlated (random splits leak). Weighted F1 is dominated by the majority class. SMOTE on synthetic data just creates more synthetic data | Provisional |
| D-03 | 2026-10-07 | O2 compares against baselines: persistence-with-base-rates, a rule heuristic, and a Markov chain; plus an oracle ceiling using the hidden traits | Shows whether ML adds anything, and how close it comes to the best possible score | Provisional |
| D-04 | 2026-10-09 | **Bands: Green `< 30%`, Yellow `30% to < 60%`, Red `≥ 60%`** | FYP1 contradicted itself at exactly 60%. PIDM states banks generally accept DSR *below* 60%, so 60% itself is not acceptable | Final (provisional pending supervisor) |
| D-05 | 2026-10-07 | Hysteresis: enter Red at ≥ 60%, leave only below 55%; DSR uses the median income of the last 3 months; an "insufficient income data" state replaces the band when that income is very low | Stops bands flapping for gig workers; avoids dividing by zero | Provisional |
| D-06 | 2026-10-09 | **DSR uses net income** (employees: gross − 11% EPF − SOCSO/EIS; gig: income as received) | Matches common Malaysian bank practice | Final (provisional pending supervisor) |
| D-07 | 2026-10-07 | **DTI = total outstanding debt ÷ annual net income** (separate from DSR) | FYP1's synthetic data computed DTI identically to DSR (a bug) | Final |
| D-08 | 2026-10-09 | **Herding detection dropped.** Remaining behavioural proxies: overconfidence, impulsivity, **delayed correction** (renamed from loss aversion) | Herding is social imitation by definition, and transaction data carries no social signal. | D-09 | 2026-10-07 | Festive and semester-start months are excluded from or adjusted in the behavioural flags | Prevents false impulsivity flags (Raya, CNY, Deepavali, Christmas, start of semester) | Provisional |
