# FAST AI Furniture Asset Prompt

Use this prompt with an image-generation model.

---

Create a high-quality transparent PNG isometric pixel-art asset sheet for a mobile financial gamification game called **FAST**.

## Art Direction

- 2D isometric pixel art
- consistent 2:1 isometric perspective
- designed to visually fit a 64 × 32 isometric tile grid
- cozy, clean, modern but modest
- affordable Malaysian young-adult starter-apartment feeling
- appropriate for students, young workers, and first-time renters
- subtle Malaysian residential influence without stereotypes
- warm tropical color palette
- clean readable silhouettes suitable for smartphone screens
- consistent lighting direction, scale, outlines, shading, and pixel density
- no realistic 3D rendering
- no photorealism
- transparent background
- every asset isolated with generous transparent spacing
- no overlapping objects
- no text
- no labels
- no numbers
- no logos
- no UI
- no people
- no watermarks

## Important Technical Requirements

All furniture must share the same:

- isometric perspective
- visual scale
- lighting direction
- outline thickness
- pixel density

Objects may be taller than their floor footprint, but their base must visually align with a 64 × 32 isometric grid.

Do not generate structural floor or wall tiles in this prompt.

## Starter Furniture

Include:

- modest single bed with simple bedding
- wardrobe
- compact study/work desk
- office chair
- small two-seat sofa
- coffee table
- compact refrigerator
- simple kitchen counter
- sink unit
- simple storage cabinet
- small dining chair
- small dining table
- shoe rack
- basic lamp
- small rug

## FAST Interactive Objects

Include:

- budget board
- savings plant
- scanner / receipt station
- financial-risk warning lamp
- small phone/device station for AI assistant access
- coin jar or savings container

## Malaysian Context

Use subtle details appropriate for a modest modern Malaysian apartment:

- compact practical furniture
- tropical-friendly indoor plants
- practical kitchen design
- shoe storage near the entrance
- warm Southeast Asian apartment atmosphere

Avoid exaggerated cultural symbols.

## Overall Mood

The apartment should feel:

- affordable
- practical
- young
- slightly cozy
- lived-in
- optimistic

Do not make it:

- luxurious
- futuristic
- extravagant
- fantasy-themed

Generate reusable isolated game assets only, not a complete apartment scene.
